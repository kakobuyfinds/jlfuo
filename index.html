Category notes for a more useful Kakobuy spreadsheet
Category Fit & material notes Details to compare
Shoes Save the sneaker size chart and available shoe measurements for the selected size. Keep a colorway comparison and close-ups of outsole details with the product link.
Hoodies/Sweaters Record hoodie measurements or sweater fabric information; include sleeve length. Check the listing's knitwear care instructions before deciding how the item fits your routine.
T-Shirts For cotton T-shirts, record shoulder width and chest measurement using the seller's method. Review graphic print quality in close-up images and note any missing information.
Jackets Compare outerwear sizing with enough room for your preferred layered outfits. Request clear views of the jacket lining and zipper details if they matter to your choice.
Pants/Shorts Record pants waist measurement, trouser length or shorts inseam as appropriate. Check the stated fabric stretch and whether the waist is measured relaxed or stretched.
Headwear For baseball caps and other adjustable headwear, note the stated hat circumference. Look at the cap closure and record the adjustment range when the listing provides it.
Sets For matching sets, record top and bottom measurements separately; check tracksuit sizing. Compare the colors of coordinated outfits and confirm which pieces are included in two-piece sets.
Underwear/underpants Review underwear fabric, boxer brief sizing and notes relevant to waistband comfort. Read the seller's hygiene policy and confirm pack quantities before adding an item to your shortlist.
Jersey For football jerseys, check jersey sizing against a garment with a similar fit. Record name printing choices and inspect badge details for the selected version.
Accessories Keep bracelet sizing, stated jewelry materials and relevant accessory dimensions together. Inspect clasp details and ask the seller to clarify any material description that is unclear.

Use “needs review” when a measurement or material is unknown. A blank field should stay a question until the listing or seller supplies an answer.

02 / Make the shortlist useful

From Kakobuy finds
to considered choices.

Separate what caught your attention from what you have checked. These three habits keep your research easier to revisit.

01

Build your Kakobuy shoes spreadsheet

Save the exact product link, selected color and size. Record the listing's fit information and a checked date. Compare similar pairs using the same measurements, keeping any unresolved sizing questions visible.

02

Shape your Kakobuy clothing spreadsheet

Group garments by category, then record the dimensions that affect how you wear them. Keep tops and bottoms separate when comparing sets. Add fabric and care notes where the listing provides them.

03

Keep the full cost in view

Record the item price with its currency and checked date. Track shipping, service charges and any applicable import costs separately as you confirm them. Treat an early total as an estimate until all charges are known.

04 / Clear answers

A few things
worth knowing.

Quick answers about building a shortlist, checking fit and using this guide.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a list used to organize product links and shopping notes for Kakobuy-related research. Useful columns include category, product URL, selected option, measurements, price checked and QC notes. This page is an independent planning guide with links to CAFANS categories.

How do I start a Kakobuy shoes spreadsheet?

Start with a product URL, model or style description, chosen color and size, and the listing's measurement information. Add comparison notes and the date you checked the listing. Keep items with missing fit information marked as needing review.

What belongs in a Kakobuy clothing spreadsheet?

Record the garment category, selected size, fabric information, chest or waist measurement, length and relevant seller notes. Compare measurements with a similar garment you own, using the same measuring method.

Do QC photos prove authenticity or guarantee fit?

QC photos can help you inspect visible details such as stitching, color, labels and measurements. They do not by themselves establish authenticity, confirm every material claim or guarantee how an item will fit.

Are the brand images live CAFANS product listings?

The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès images are editorial style references. Follow a category link to CAFANS and check the destination listing for the actual item, seller information, options, price and availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

This page provides a category guide and a copyable column structure. Use the Copy column headings button to paste the headings into your own spreadsheet. The page does not provide a live product feed or a downloadable product database.
